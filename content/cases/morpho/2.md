## Summary

A related-party control problem appeared in the AZND/USDC Morpho market. Ownership of the collateral asset was concentrated among a small group, while capital from a USDC vault was allocated to the market and borrowed against that collateral.

## Example

Rather than sourcing an external market price, the oracle effectively treated AZND as worth a fixed amount of USDC.

The fixed price was a warning sign, but it was not itself the exploit. The broader problem was concentrated control across the collateral asset, market and vault allocation. Depositors were the only party outside that shared economic structure.
